# EnergyShift
## The Changing Global Energy System

### 1. Project Name
**The Changing Global Energy System: Energy Transition, Energy Security & Critical Minerals**

### 2. Project Idea
This graduation project aims to analyze the changing global energy system by exploring the relationships between renewable energy growth, fossil fuel dependency, energy security, critical minerals, energy storage, and electricity demand.

Using seven interactive Power BI dashboards, the project will investigate whether renewable energy expansion is associated with reduced fossil fuel dependence, compare energy security across countries, and examine how critical mineral production concentration and commodity price fluctuations may influence the global energy transition.

The project focuses on discovering meaningful patterns, relationships, and differences through data analysis rather than simply reporting trends or presenting assumptions.

**Main Topics:**
- Global Energy Overview
- Global Energy Transition
- Energy Security
- Critical Minerals

### 4. Work Plan
The project will follow these main phases:

1. **Research and Data Collection:** Identify reliable data sources and collect relevant global energy, economic, and mineral data.
2. **Data Preparation:** Clean, transform, and validate the datasets using suitable data preparation tools.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigate trends, comparisons, and relationships across countries and over time.
4. **Data Modeling:** Build an appropriate data model for analysis and reporting in Power BI.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Design and develop seven interactive dashboards covering the project's main topics.
6. **Insights and Validation:** Interpret findings, validate comparisons, and document evidence-based insights.
7. **Finalization and Presentation:** Complete project documentation, prepare the final presentation, and organize project deliverables.

### 5. Roles & Responsibilities
Team responsibilities will be finalized based on the agreed division of work.

### 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The final KPIs will depend on data availability and the analytical requirements of each dashboard. Candidate indicators include:

- Renewable Energy Share (%)
- Fossil Fuel Share (%)
- Renewable Energy Growth Rate (%)
- Energy Consumption per Capita
- Energy Import Dependency (%)
- Energy Production and Consumption
- Critical Mineral Production by Country
- Critical Mineral Price Trends
- Energy Storage Capacity
- Electricity Demand Growth (%)

These indicators will be reviewed and refined during the data preparation and analysis phases.
